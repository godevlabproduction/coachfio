Liquid Glass,
the Coachfio cut.

Apple's current material design (iOS 26 era): surfaces behave like lensed glass - they blur and saturate what's behind them, catch light on the top rim, keep concentric corner geometry, and respond with springy, morphing motion. This page is the material itself, live. Toggle the sun to see it adapt.

2 · Concentric geometry

radius = parent − padding

Every nested radius is the parent's radius minus the gap between them. Corners stay parallel at every level - this is what makes glass read as machined, not drawn.

3 · Controls

Interaction is springy and physical: controls scale up on hover, compress on press, and the segmented thumb morphs between options.
